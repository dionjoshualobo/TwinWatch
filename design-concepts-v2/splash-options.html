Reference notes and sources

Statue: high confidence replica/cast of the Venus de’ Medici, not the armless Venus de Milo. Identifiers: both arms present, right hand across the breast, left hand lowered, slight lean, head turned, hair in a bun at the nape, pale marble/plaster.

Seating: the “couch” is two separate Art Deco open-arm club chairs pushed together, with exposed polished cherry-toned wood bars on the arms and a visible center division. A third, more overstuffed chair faces them. Generic sofa silhouettes, plush rolled arms, and one continuous three-seat body are wrong.

Tree: two distinct motifs: Glastonbury Grove is a circle of leafless sycamores with a black pool and red curtain; the speaking tree is a bare dead tree with electrical crackle and a pale organic pod/mouth. These options do not merge them.
