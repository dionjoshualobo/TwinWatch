# Watch Together — app icon concepts

Six vector concepts for the app icon. Each has a 512x512 PNG and editable SVG. The contact sheet is also exported as PNG and PDF.

- `01-dual-play` — two overlapping play marks: watching as a pair/group.
- `02-shared-frame` — shared screen with two members and a play action.
- `03-orbit-play` — central play core with orbiting friends/activity.
- `04-episode-stack` — stacked episode cards with a checkmark for watched episodes.
- `05-together-w` — abstract W monogram built from two play paths.
- `06-watched-check` — play button transformed by a watched confirmation check.

Palette: Ink #12161C, Paper #F5F0E6, Amber #FFB454, Mint #6EE7C3, Coral #FF806B.
